# NNNN. Short title in the imperative

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-NNNN (link it when that happens)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** who agreed to this

## Context

What forces are at play? What made this a decision rather than an obvious
choice? Include the constraint that actually bit -- a server's behaviour, a
protocol's rule, a failure that happened. If the decision was driven by
evidence, cite it: the measurement, the log line, the server version.

## Decision

What was decided, stated plainly and in the present tense.

## Consequences

What this costs, not only what it buys. Name the cases where this is the wrong
answer, and what someone should do when they hit one. An ADR whose consequences
section is all upside is a advertisement, not a record.

## Alternatives considered

What else was on the table, and the specific reason each was rejected. "It was
worse" is not a reason; "it fails closed on a network partition" is.
